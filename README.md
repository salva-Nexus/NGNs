# Salva NGNs — Digital Naira Stablecoin

> A blockchain-native, 1:1 Naira-pegged digital currency built for speed, transparency, and zero friction.

---

## What is Salva NGNs?

Salva NGNs is a digital currency permanently pegged **1:1 to the Nigerian Naira**, deployed on modern blockchain infrastructure. It is not a speculative asset — it does not fluctuate, surge, or crash like Bitcoin or Ethereum.

**1 NGNs = 1 Nigerian Naira. Always.**

NGNs moves the Naira off restrictive legacy banking rails and onto a high-speed, internet-native network — making the Naira as programmable and borderless as any modern digital asset.

---

## Why NGNs Exists

Traditional banking infrastructure and peer-to-peer (P2P) trading in Nigeria carry significant friction:

- Daily transaction limits that block large settlements
- Delayed transfers and unpredictable confirmation times
- Manual payment verification with no transparent audit trail
  
NGNs was purpose-built to eliminate these bottlenecks — turning the Naira into a programmable, internet-native asset that settles in seconds with complete on-chain proof.

---

## Core Utility

### ⚡ Zero-Friction Automated Trading
Interact directly with automated smart contract systems like **Salva V3**. Swap NGNs for digital dollars (USDC/USDT) instantly — no manual bank matching, no account number exchanges, no waiting for credit alerts.

### 🕐 Seconds to Settle
Blockchain settlement in under **2 seconds**, with immutable, transparent proof of every transaction. No disputed payments. No hanging transfers during peak banking hours.

### 💸 Negligible Transaction Fees
Move millions of Naira across wallets or across borders for a fraction of a single Kobo in network fees — no hidden charges, no remittance markups.

### 🔒 Zero FX Anxiety
Unlike holding volatile assets, NGNs carries **no foreign exchange risk**. Merchants and businesses can operate at blockchain speed while pricing entirely in their home currency.

## Ecosystem Safety

To protect users and local commerce, NGNs includes built-in protocol-level safety features:

- **Pause mechanism** — operations can be halted instantly in the event of an exploit or security threat
- **Targeted freeze capability** — specific malicious actors can be isolated without disrupting the broader ecosystem
- **On-chain transparency** — every mint, transfer, and burn is publicly verifiable

These guardrails ensure that digital storefronts and businesses running on Salva remain secure and trustworthy for everyday users.
